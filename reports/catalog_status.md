SR\* is the Sharpe the best of N random trials would be expected to reach by chance. Any candidate must clear **0.0865** before its Sharpe means anything, and that bar rises with every trial spent — including the 72 spent on F07, which could not have produced evidence.

> **Integrity gap: 117 trials are counted from a report rather than from a cell file.** MGC's 117 cells were run and reported but never written to reports/f03_cells.json. Worse, **`trials.jsonl` was never written at all**: `src/futuresres/stats/trials.py` — the hash-chained append-only log — was ported from the crypto repo with its tests, but no Stage 1 runner calls it. N above is reconstructed from output files, which is exactly the reconstruction the append-only log exists to make unnecessary. Every future Stage 1 run must write to it.
