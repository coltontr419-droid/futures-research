SR\* is the Sharpe the best of N random trials would be expected to reach by chance. Any candidate must clear **0.1395** before its Sharpe means anything, and that bar rises with every trial spent - including the 72 spent on F07, which could not have produced evidence.

**N now comes from `trials.jsonl`, whose chain verifies.** It used to be reconstructed by counting rows in output files, which could not detect a deleted trial and silently lost anything never persisted. All 342 records predating the wiring are marked `reconstructed`: their timestamps are the backfill's, their within-run ordering is whatever the output file held, and nothing proves those files were not edited between the run and the backfill. They count toward N - a look at the data is a look at the data - but they are not evidence that a log was being kept.

**102 firing-rate measurements are chained separately in `measurements.jsonl` and are NOT in N.** They count how often a condition triggers and compute no return series, so they could never produce a candidate. Including them would raise SR\* - a stricter bar, which sounds safe, but a bar set by a category error is not conservative. See `reports/decisions.md` §16.
