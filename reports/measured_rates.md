**Nothing here is Stage 1 and nothing here spends a trial.** These are counts of how often each condition triggers on real data.

Declared rates no longer gate anything. §21: F02 declared one firing per session, the gate cleared it on that basis, and the run produced 106-707 events per cell against a predicted 4,006-4,125. No test could have caught it, because a declaration has no independent source to check against. A hypothesis absent from this file is **unschedulable**.
